Notes for the raid leader

  • Ground: three camps within 15 yards of their priest — Gas Nova is Mass Dispelled the instant she starts casting. Tank never faces the raid (Cleave).
  • Encapsulate: the lifted player runs away from everyone; Divine Shield / Ice Block / Cloak end it. Raid at 100% before it lands — this is the wipe mechanic.
  • Corrosion on the tank: cooldowns and trinkets, tank healers on high alert. Fungal Decay is a disease — cleanse.
  • Air: move to the middle, run the green beam (Fog of Corruption) in a circle away from the raid, then stack for the three breath lanes. Off-tank picks up the undead from the fog. DPS wait for threat when she lands.
